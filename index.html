Why the south pole is hard

Near 90°S, the Sun stays low and skims a jagged horizon. Ridges a few kilometers away can block sunlight for days, and Earth may sit just above or below the local terrain — so power and communications rise and fall together with landing site and date.

This Learn view uses the same scenario engine as Planner. Use Rank sites and Suggest better dates below to see why one site/date wins and another fails — then adopt with one click.

Try CR1 vs SR1 on the same week, then slip the start — small date moves can flip which site looks healthier. Verdict grades the mission window; scrub explores instants inside it.

On Earth, a clear day usually means continuous sunlight and radio. Near the lunar pole, sunlight and Earth line-of-sight can vanish for hours even when “day” should last — that is the engineering surprise this tool teaches.

Mission power combines panel generation with constant-load battery dispatch. Inspect unmet Wh and electrical outages, then compare powered Earth contact against the mission requirements. The ? controls explain each calculation.

Provenance & limits

  • Horizons: bundled JPL observer data for CR1/SR1 (Jan 1–Mar 1 boundary, 2027); uncovered or unverified solar inputs stay unknown — source labeled on scrub readout.
  • Horizons (terrain): LOLA 5 m near-field + 320 m overview for CR1/SR1 only; gaps never invented.
  • Regional “Find best location” uses a ~10 km polar-cap grid with LOLA 320 m overview horizons (2° bins) — coarse ranking, not a meter-perfect global optimum.
  • Custom / curated / Apollo / CLPS catalog entries use an optimistic sphere horizon — labeled explicitly; do not treat as LOLA-grade.
  • Rank / date / search scores use the same assessMission engine as the badges; non-study catalog sites remain sphere-optimistic.
  • 3D markers: coral ■ chosen (Site A), amber ◆ search hits, teal ● catalog, violet ◎ Site B. Default terrain layer is mesh/wireframe.
  • 3D display lighting ≠ authoritative observer Sun/Earth status. Quest VR runtime is not shipped — export JSON for external viz.
